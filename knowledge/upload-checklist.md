# Art Lab Studio — Video Upload Checklist

Sample document for the Art Lab knowledge base. The studio and every rule here are fictional.

## Before publishing

1. Upload the video as Unlisted first and leave it for a 24-hour review by a second team member.
2. Upload human-reviewed English captions as an .srt file. Never publish with auto-captions only.
3. Add at least 3 chapter markers. The first chapter must start at 00:00.
4. Add an end screen for the last 20 seconds, pointing to one related video.
5. Use no more than 15 tags.

## Thumbnail

The thumbnail is 1280x720 pixels and under 2 MB. Check it on a phone screen before publishing.

## Description

Every description has, in this order: the disclosure line if sponsored, a two-sentence summary,
the timestamps, and a links section with gear and affiliate links.

## When to publish

We publish on Tuesday or Thursday at 17:00 CET. Shorts can go out any day, but never within
two hours of a long video.
