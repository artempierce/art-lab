# Art Lab Studio — Sponsorship & Disclosure Policy

Sample document for the Art Lab knowledge base. The studio and every rule here are fictional.

## Why we have this policy

Viewers trust us because we say what we think. Sponsorships pay for equipment and editing time, but
they must never cost us that trust. This policy says which deals we take and how we disclose them.

## Disclosure rules

- Every sponsored video must disclose the sponsorship out loud within the first 30 seconds.
- The first line of the description must start with "#ad" followed by the sponsor's name.
- The YouTube "includes paid promotion" box is always ticked for sponsored videos, with no exceptions.
- Affiliate links are marked "(affiliate)" right after the link in the description.

## What we accept

- At most 2 sponsors per video.
- A sponsor segment may be no longer than 90 seconds.
- The minimum fee is $1,500 per integration, paid net-30 after the video goes live.
- The sponsor gets one review round of the script, for factual accuracy about their product only.
  They never get approval over our opinions, the edit, or the video's conclusion.

## What we refuse

We do not accept sponsorships from gambling, crypto trading, or vaping companies, whatever the fee.
We also refuse any deal that asks us to hide the sponsorship or to review a product we have not used
for at least two weeks.

## Who handles contracts

All sponsorship contracts are reviewed by Maya Chen, our partnerships lead, before anyone signs.
Questions about an ongoing deal go to Maya first, then to the channel owner.
