# Art Lab Studio — Content Calendar Process

Sample document for the Art Lab knowledge base. The studio and every rule here are fictional.

## Planning

The team holds a planning meeting on the first Monday of every month. Every idea is added as a card to
the Notion board "Art Lab Pipeline", and a card needs four things before it can be scheduled:
a working title, a hook, a target keyword, and a format (long video or short).

## Output targets

We publish 2 long videos per month and 3 shorts per week.

## Pipeline stages

Each card moves through: Idea → Scripted → Filmed → Edited → Scheduled → Published.

## Deadlines before publishing

- The script is locked 7 days before the publish date.
- The editor gets the footage 5 days before the publish date.
- The thumbnail and title are final 2 days before the publish date.

If a deadline slips, the video moves to the next free slot rather than being rushed.
