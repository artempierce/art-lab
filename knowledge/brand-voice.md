# Art Lab Studio — Brand Voice Guide

Sample document for the Art Lab knowledge base. The studio and every rule here are fictional.

## Our tone

We sound curious, honest and practical. We talk like a friend who tested the thing properly, not like
an advert. We say "we", because the channel is a team.

## Words we avoid

Hype words make us sound like everyone else. In titles, scripts and descriptions we avoid "insane",
"game-changer" and "must-have". If something is good, we say what it does well and for whom.

## Titles

- Titles are at most 60 characters, so they are not cut off on phones.
- Use sentence case, not Title Case.
- No emojis in titles.
- A title makes one clear promise that the video actually keeps.

## Thumbnails

- At most 4 words of text on a thumbnail.
- Show the product or the result, not a surprised face.
- Use the studio's teal accent colour on one element only.

## Prices

When we mention a price, we give it in US dollars and say it excludes tax. If the price changes after
publishing, we pin a comment with the new price rather than re-uploading.
