# Art Lab Studio — Equipment & Expenses Policy

Sample document for the Art Lab knowledge base. The studio and every rule here are fictional.

## Who approves a purchase

- Under $300: no approval needed; just keep the receipt.
- From $300 to $1,500: approval from our producer, Jonas Weber, before buying.
- Above $1,500: approval from the channel owner, with 2 quotes from different sellers.

## Receipts and reimbursement

Receipts are submitted within 14 days of purchase, in the shared "Expenses" sheet.
Approved expenses are reimbursed on the 5th of the following month.

## Review units

Review units sent by brands are returned within 30 days unless the brand says in writing that we can
keep them. Kept units are listed in the description of the video that features them.

## Travel

We book economy flights only. Hotels are capped at $180 per night. Meals on shoot days are covered up
to $45 per person per day.
