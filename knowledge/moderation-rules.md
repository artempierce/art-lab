# Art Lab Studio — Community & Comment Moderation Rules

Sample document for the Art Lab knowledge base. The studio and every rule here are fictional.

## What moderation is for

Comments are where viewers help each other. Moderation keeps that space useful and kind. It is not a
tool for hiding criticism: we never delete criticism of us that is not abusive.

## Daily routine

- Pin a top comment within the first hour after publishing.
- Reply to at least 20 comments in the first 24 hours.
- Delete spam links within 12 hours of them being posted.

## The three-strike rule

1. First strike: a public or private warning.
2. Second strike: a 7-day timeout, using "hide user from channel" and removing it after a week.
3. Third strike: permanent block.

Threats, doxxing or hate speech skip the strikes and lead straight to a permanent block and a report.

## The moderation team

Two volunteer moderators help the studio. The team meets for a weekly sync on Mondays to review hard
cases and update the list of held words.
